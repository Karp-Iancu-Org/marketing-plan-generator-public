## Example consultation route: an offer question

For “How should we improve this offer?”, identify the niche and open its ignored intake record first. Then search [`text/offers.txt`](text/offers.txt) for `Value Equation`; it routes to PDF pp. 82–84, which frames dream outcome, perceived likelihood, time delay, and effort/sacrifice. If the intake lacks the desired result, evidence, delivery timing, or required customer effort, ask for those facts before proposing an offer change. Cite `$100M Offers`, PDF page(s) used, and label the recommendation separately from confirmed business evidence.
