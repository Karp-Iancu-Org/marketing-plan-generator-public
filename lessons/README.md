## Applying the lessons

Translate the workshop principles only after the new niche's intake and research produce evidence. Do not invent worked examples, reuse another client's facts, or carry a previous plan's numbers into a new one. The frameworks, numbers, Q&A prescriptions, and benchmarks are starting points to test against the new niche.
