# Workshop Lessons — Master Index & Deduped Prescription List (Niche-Agnostic)

Nine sessions, Aug 19–20, 2026, Acquisition.com/Mozi marketing workshop. One breakdown doc per session (01–09). This index is the deduped list of every operative rule and number, with pointers to the doc that carries full context. It is the rulebook the plan templates apply.

## The three shifts everything else follows from (docs 01, 02, 03, 09)

1. **Social media is dead; interest media is alive.** The algorithm distributes by interest, not follower count — "content is the targeting." Niche content that reaches 250 exactly-right people beats viral reach. Followers, views, virality: explicitly not business metrics (ACQ tripled views and revenue fell 3×; reversed the strategy, views fell 3× and revenue tripled).
2. **Meta Andromeda (~Oct 2025): the ad creative IS the targeting.** Interest/demographic targeting matters less every day; Meta matches each creative to the "pocket" of people it resonates with. Consequence: far more creatives, far narrower call-outs, much shorter creative lifespan — local creative burns out fastest of all.
3. **AI gives infinite scale.** ACQ's highlights channel: ~20 posts/day by 1 person + workflows, more attributed revenue than the flagship channel. Threads: 24 posts/day fully automated → +903K followers. "You want to do so much volume it would be unreasonable to fail."

## Creative volume & assembly (docs 01, 03, 08, 09)

- **10× rule:** the spend that used 15 ads pre-Andromeda needs ~150 now. Rule of thumb: **~10 new creatives/week per $10K/month spend** (creatives/week ≈ monthly spend / $1,000).
- **Assembly, not creation: hook × meat × CTA.** 50 hooks × 3–5 meats × 1–3 CTAs ≈ 750 ads from one shoot.
- **70/20/10:** 70% minimal variations of winners, 20% adjacent changes, 10% wild new (a budget you expect to burn; new super-winners come from here and re-enter the cycle as the new base).
- **10-to-1:** one big idea, ten reasons, five expression modes (stories, examples, metaphors, data, statistics). "We get bored before the market does."
- **Call-out laddering:** category → sub-niche → sub-sub-niche (plumber → drain-cleaning plumber). Ten narrow ads beat one broad ad.
- **5 awareness levels × 5 hook types:** unaware→curiosity, problem-aware→pain, solution-aware→promise, product-aware→proof, most-aware→offer. (The canonical unaware example: the mesothelioma ad.)
- **You cannot predict winners** — predicted winners bomb, "this sucks" ads win. Volume + algorithm decide.

## Campaign mechanics (doc 08 — the operational core)

- **Test ABO** (one concept → 5–10 variations → one ad set with its own budget) → **scale CBO** (duplicate winners into one CBO campaign per angle).
- **Broad audiences** (no interest targeting) + optional lookalikes + **CAPI** from the CRM/backend as the third data layer.
- **Optimize on qualified/valuable events only:** for lead-gen, 3–5 objective qualifying questions with the **pixel firing after qualification** and the **two-identical-thank-you-pages** trick (keep every lead; train the pixel only on good ones). The same principle generalizes: optimize on trial/activation, not installs; on purchases, not add-to-carts.
- **Golden BB:** a good creative that flops may have trained on the wrong pocket — kill it and re-upload into a fresh ad set (once).
- **Offer must match avatar expectations** (a $2K roof to $1M-home owners sells zero; a $20K self-checkout to cold traffic sells zero — bridge offers exist for a reason).

## Organic→paid flywheel (docs 02, 03)

- Post organic at volume → find the ~1-in-10 outliers → run them as ads (the $100M book launch: 4,000 purpose-shot ads all lost to organic clips with one appended CTA line).
- **Saves** is the #1 leading revenue KPI for educator content (across 1,000+ accounts).
- **Capture, don't manufacture:** look / set up ("an hour of pre is worth ten of post") / capture / edit (Hook-Retain-Reward).
- **Life-cycle ads** (5 checkpoints: sales-call doubts → onboarding → first win → 6-month → 1-year) — the best campaign Gym Launch ever ran; the customer's own objections become the hooks.
- Testimonial arc: 6 scripted questions; **the bridge (their doubt → why they said yes anyway) is the conversion mechanism.** Post ALL testimonials; repost winners forever.
- CTA doctrine: spell out mechanics, demonstrate on screen, then urgency/scarcity/bonus. No lazy CTAs; organic gets CTAs too.

## Funnel & sales motion (docs 07, 08, 05)

- **Speed-to-lead: contact within 60 seconds.** Nurture: double-dial + text 2×/day days 1–3, then 1×/day through day 7. Book/convert within 3 days; day-before "we reserved this for you" touch.
- **VSL before every step** (5/7/10 minutes, scripted), with the coffee-enforcement script. The service-business version: mine sales/consult recordings for the 10–15 most-asked questions → the expert answers them in a pre-call VSL → the call becomes pure discovery to commitment.
- **One funnel for warm and cold** ("hope for warm, prepare for cold"); 70/30 prospect/rep talk ratio; kill zombies (front-load known objections); the **"Why People Buy"** transcript-mining loop feeds hooks and content weekly.
- **Lead scoring:** best leads → best closers (a 15–20% lift without another ad dollar).
- **Close-rate → pricing:** ≥75% close = 3× price; ~60% dialed = +50%; **40% is the healthy target.**
- **Flow → monetize → THEN friction.** And the self-liquidating front end ("client-financed acquisition"): a paid low-ticket step that covers ad cost makes cold traffic effectively free.

## Proof, brand, positioning (docs 02, 03, 06)

- SPCL: status/power/credibility/likeness — **power (say-do correspondence) and credibility (proof) are what matter for educators.** Show what only you can show.
- **Brand is the only thing that lowers CAC over time.**
- Biggest fish, smallest pond; "the missed opportunity is not a problem to solve."
- **Avatar-specific landing pages:** the page continues the ad's exact conversation — a video speaking only to that avatar, one action, minimal exit points; **subdomains are fine.**
- Entity trust: "we/us/my team" language + team spotlights transfer founder trust to the company. People follow people, not logos.
- Audit every touchpoint (the Lorem-Ipsum thank-you page that silently killed show rates); anonymous internal brand survey; written brand guidelines before anyone represents the brand.
- **Key-person risk (doc 05):** the client-ad flywheel ended with customers in 80% of top ads and the founder in zero of the top 40 — "your future customers want to look at your current customers."

## AI production system (docs 04, 09 — the asset project's blueprint)

- **Vibe editing:** describe the edit to Claude instead of operating an editor. The clipping automation: Claude Code + ffmpeg + Whisper transcription + LLM clip selection; brand interview (colors, subtitle style); 90 min of footage → 7 clips in minutes; 35 posts/day at $2.59/video vs $10K/video mainline.
- Build method: reverse-prompt the roadmap → plan mode → make the agent interview you → engineer the clip-selection prompt (extract first principles from proven clips) → review the plan → iterate. Shorts need stricter prompts than mid-form.
- **Name your hooks** so AI can find them in long footage. Effective production beats overproduction; raw talking-to-camera is a top format and the easiest to automate.
- The full chain (scrape → themes → scripts → record [the only human step] → detect → edit → post → performance loop), replicated per platform.
- The 5-skill creative workflow (doc 04): hook generation → competitor ad-library mining → pattern-finding → 70/20/10 permutation → platform formatting.
- Proof at data scale: 11,000 sales recordings mined → front-end rewritten → ~40% lift; webinar transcripts mined → show rate 2.7%→30%.

## Local / professional-services specifics (docs 05, 06, 07, 08)

- Legal-services case study: CPL $183 → $65 via rewritten hooks + **10 new static images** + VSL/script matched to avatar voice — "professionals rarely advertise well, so it's easy to win in a local market."
- Services case study: profile the **top 10% of customers**, rewrite ads+scripts to them → 20%→61% appointment conversion; higher CPC, lower CAC; compare LTV:CAC per channel and double down on the winner.
- Multi-location answer: geo-targeted front-end offers only into weak locations.
- Retroactive avatar method (when customer data exists): rank by LTV, top 20%, trait commonalities — and if you don't know why they bought, **call them**.
