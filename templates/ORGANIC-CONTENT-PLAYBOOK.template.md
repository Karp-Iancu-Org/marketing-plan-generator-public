- **Review/rating of the week:** screenshot static from {{PRAISE_SOURCE}} <!-- source: intake §8.3 --> (name shortened as published, one sentence highlighted) + an on-camera review-reaction variant (read it, add 30 seconds of context). Renewable weekly.
- **Anonymized retells** <!-- keep if the niche has privacy sensitivity (intake §3.2); else replace with straight customer stories -->: a team member retells a real customer arc on the 6-question skeleton — who they were → before (external/internal) → **the bridge (the doubt, and why they proceeded anyway — the conversion mechanism)** → after → advice-to-someone-unsure.
- **Testimonial and life-cycle clips** as `../04-creative-production/CAPTURE-SYSTEMS.md` produces them, at the customer's consent rung.

- **C2 save/send:** "Save this for when you need it — and send it to the friend who does." (The save IS the KPI; ask for it.)
- **C3 follow:** "Follow for {{FOLLOW_PROMISE}}" <!-- source: positioning — the content promise, e.g. "straight answers about X" --> (soft; education posts only.)
Congruence: the link matches the avatar in the post — an avatar-specific post links its avatar's page, never the homepage.

## 6. The weekly operating ritual (60–90 min, same day every week — owner: {{RITUAL_OWNER}} <!-- source: intake §9.4 -->)

1. **Score** (per `MEASUREMENT-ORGANIC.md`): pull saves, save-rate, buying-question DMs/comments, and link taps per post for the trailing 7–14 days.
2. **Outlier check:** any post ≥3× the account's trailing-30-day median (saves preferred, views acceptable) is an outlier → append ONE CTA line (end-card, voiceover, or caption — never re-edit the body) → hand to the paid test queue named `ORG-<post-id>`. Non-negotiable: every outlier gets tried as paid ("next time you do a viral video and you don't run it as an ad, we're not friends anymore" — lesson 03).
3. **Repost queue:** any proof/education winner past its 6–8 week cycle goes back out.
4. **Schedule next week** from the pipeline's rendered queue: pillar mix ≈ 60/25/10/5, every post tagged avatar + series + hook type in the manifest.
5. **Backlog groom:** new harvest questions → next shoot's run-sheet.
